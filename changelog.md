## 2.1.0

The mod has been rebuilt from the ground up.

### New
- Now available for Minecraft 26.1.2
- New modules:
  - **Reach**: reach blocks and entities from further away
  - **Step Assist**: walk up full blocks without jumping (can be toggled)
  - **Phantom Ward**: phantoms stop spawning for you
  - **Void Guardian**: saves you when you fall into the void
  - **Aqua Lung**: breathe underwater and mine there as fast as with Aqua Affinity
  - **Fast Build**: place blocks much faster (can be toggled)
- The Magnet module now also pulls experience orbs
- Reach distance and Fast Build speed can be changed in the config
- Other mods can now add their own modules
- All text can now be translated

### Changes and fixes
- Flight now plays nicely with other mods that let you fly
- The Night Vision module no longer shows a potion effect and no longer cancels real Night Vision potions when you toggle it off or take the ring off
- A module can no longer be installed twice on the same ring (this used to eat the module)
- The Mining module now stacks with other mining speed boosts
- The Inertia module is lighter on servers
- Removed leftover wing items that showed up in JEI

### When updating
- Your existing rings keep their modules and wings
- Module keybinds are reset to their defaults, so rebind them if you had changed them
